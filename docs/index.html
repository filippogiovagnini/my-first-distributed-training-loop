What I take from these runs

The training and validation losses fall substantially in both runs, and the larger run reaches a lower final validation loss after seeing more data. That is consistent with learning the byte-level training distribution. It does not establish that the 150M model is better solely because it is larger: it also saw more than three times as many tokens, and the experiments were not designed as a controlled comparison.

The generated text is a useful reality check. Despite the lower validation loss, the sample still loops on a phrase and does not sustain a coherent explanation. This makes a good next experiment clear: improve the evaluation protocol and compare size or data changes while holding the other variables fixed.
